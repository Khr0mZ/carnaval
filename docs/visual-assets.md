# Recursos de la nueva escenografía

Generados con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`, sin usar la API ni el CLI. Los PNG originales permanecen en el directorio de generación de Codex; las versiones WebP optimizadas y con transparencia conservada están incluidas en el sitio. Ningún retrato ni portada de la colección se ha generado o modificado.

| Recurso | Archivo final | Uso |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terciopelo bordado | `dist/assets/theatre/telon-bordado.webp` | Telones de la portada, con apertura animada; el derecho es un reflejo del mismo recurso. |
| Marco dorado | `dist/assets/theatre/marco-dorado.webp` | Molduras con relieve para programas, botones y marcos del visor. |
| Papel del programa | `dist/assets/theatre/papel-programa.webp` | Textura de fichas, entradas, álbumes y carta. |

Las imágenes son recursos decorativos, no fotografías documentales del teatro. El fondo fijo del Falla se conserva. No se añaden máscaras venecianas, confeti ni SVG decorativos.

## Prompts finales

### Telón bordado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production-ready transparent raster decoration for a Spanish carnival theatre website, Mi carnaval, inspired by the Gran Teatro Falla. Create ONE richly realistic burgundy velvet theatre curtain on the LEFT of a portrait canvas, photographed straight-on. A heavy luxurious top swag curves from the upper left down towards the upper right, gathered into a beautiful antique gold braided tieback at the left middle, with a long small gold tassel and soft heavy pleats cascading down the left edge. The curtain occupies about the left 55 percent of the canvas; the RIGHT HALF and surrounding area are genuinely TRANSPARENT so website content remains visible. Warm stage lighting, deep oxblood and garnet velvet with dimensional folds and subtly worn gold embroidered edges, refined and emotional theatrical atmosphere. The top curtain's inner edge must curve away to reveal a large empty transparent opening. Entire textile visible within the canvas, no cut-off tassel or cut-off decorative edge. This will be used at the left edge and mirrored at the right edge of a hero section. It must feel like a real theatre set, not an illustration, not a whole theatre photograph, not a web mockup. No letters, words, logos, people, faces, masks, confetti, instruments, stars or sparkles. Portrait composition approximately 2:3. Actual alpha transparency.

### Marco dorado

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent ornamental frame, a reusable nine-slice website border. A perfectly square ornate antique gold theatre programme frame inspired by the gilded decoration of the Gran Teatro Falla. Straight-on orthographic view, no perspective, no tilt. FOUR lavish sculpted acanthus and scrollwork corners in gently worn brass and champagne gold, connected by narrow straight double gold moulding rails. An elegant little symmetrical engraved flourish at the top midpoint and bottom midpoint, still entirely within the outer 14 percent border band. Realistic embossed metal relief and hand carved fine details with warm lighting and soft intrinsic shadows, restrained deep reddish antique patina in grooves. Entire frame including every outer corner is INSIDE the square image, with 2 percent transparent outer padding. The central 70 percent square is completely EMPTY and genuinely TRANSPARENT. The straight horizontal and vertical rails must be simple and repeatable, so they can stretch without distorting the ornaments at the corners. Decorative elements confined to outer 15 percent of image on each edge. Outer area and central hole transparent, actual alpha. This is a decorative border asset, not a complete design. No text, no monograms, no emblems, no crown, no masks, no confetti, no background, no paper, no photo, no wall.

### Papel de programa

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: flat texture image to be used as the material of carnival programmes, album cards and a personal letter on a website. Extreme close-up straight-on scan of beautiful warm ivory cotton rag paper, like an elegant preserved Spanish theatre programme, naturally tactile fine paper fibres and very faint irregular vertical grain. Warm cream and pale honey tones, subtly mottled edges a little warmer than the almost-uniform ivory centre, a few minute glints of embedded gold fibre but absolutely no glitter or sparkles. Clean, cared-for and precious, no grunge, no dirty stains. The whole square is filled edge-to-edge with the same continuous paper surface, no page edges, no rolled corners, no border, no frame, no shadows from objects. Centre must be very even and pale for excellent legibility of dark text. Realistic material photography with soft flat even lighting, premium printing stock. No writing, no letters, no drawings, no watermark, no objects, no decorative shapes. Square canvas.
